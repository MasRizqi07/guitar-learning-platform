SECTION 01 / DATA PHILOSOPHY
ARTICLE 5 GDPR

1. Our Educational Privacy Invariant

FretFlow Technologies is built strictly as a precision musical training tool. We operate under a fundamental organizational premise: you are our customer, not our product. We do not operate advertising syndicates, we do not monetize user usage metrics, and we do not disclose cohort learning telemetry to commercial brokers.

Every metric recorded on FretFlow exists solely to reconstruct your fretboard mastery tree, tune your spaced-repetition metronome, and calculate continuous streak dynamics. If data does not actively improve your ability to execute music on a physical instrument, we purposefully do not capture it.

SECTION 02 / INVENTORY OF TELEMETRY

2. Data We Store & Process

To deliver your dashboard metrics, persist completed fret exercises, and maintain valid account access, we collect and store the following explicit entities within encrypted cloud storage clusters:

account_circle Identity & Credentials

Email address, chosen screen identifier, and salted cryptographic hashes created via Argon2id (m=65536, t=3, p=4) or Bcrypt with cost factor 12. Plaintext credentials never exist in server memory.

bar_chart Pedagogical Telemetry

Completed lesson IDs, fretboard accuracy percentages, interval drill response latency (in milliseconds), metronome target BPMs, and cumulative daily exercise durations.

schedule Streak & Temporal Logs

Your IANA timezone designation (e.g. America/New_York) used exclusively to calculate local midnight practice roll-overs, plus active streak historical records.

devices Session Identity Hashes

Cryptographically generated pseudo-random tokens hashed via SHA-256 for active session authentication, IP address (truncated to /24 subnet for geo-security), and User-Agent headers.

SECTION 05 / AUTHENTICATION & CREDENTIALS

5. Session Security & Token Cryptography

FretFlow issues high-entropy cryptographically generated random tokens (256-bit CSPRNG) upon successful authentication. Tokens are stored client-side in __Host- prefixed, HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict secure cookies, immune to JavaScript XSS retrieval.

lock_reset Session Revocation Engine

You can terminate individual hardware sessions or trigger remote multi-device de-authentication instantly from your Security Settings.

fingerprint Salted PostgreSQL Hashing

We persist only the SHA-256 digest of active session tokens in our PostgreSQL primary database. If database dumps are intercepted, sessions cannot be reconstructed.
